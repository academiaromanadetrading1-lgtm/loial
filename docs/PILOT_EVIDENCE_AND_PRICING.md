# LOIAL — pilot, evidence and pricing

## First 100 businesses
Recruit across food service, retail and services. Participation starts on the existing free plan (50 customers); do not promise unlimited free usage unless approved separately.

Record onboarding date, business category, unique enrolled customers, distinct verified visits, rewards redeemed and observation windows. Exclude test accounts, staff demos and duplicate scans. Define a returning customer as a unique customer with at least two verified visits in the same observation window. Compare equal-length windows only where comparable baseline data exist; report counts, denominator, missing records and seasonality. No causal claims from uncontrolled before/after comparisons.

Publish case studies only after explicit business consent for names, logos, quotes and aggregate metrics. No customer identities. Never offer rewards for public reviews. A case-study statement must be supported by recorded observations and the firm's approval.

## Price decision
Existing Start (0 EUR, 50 customers) and ProBusiness (67 EUR/month) remain unchanged. Evaluate a proposed intermediate 19–29 EUR plan for 200–300 customers in interviews; neither price nor entitlement is approved. Measure conversion at the free cap, reasons for abandonment and willingness to pay. Model payment fees, AI costs, hosting, support, refunds, taxes and ambassador commission before activation.

The current 20+10 EUR first-payment commission exceeds a 19 EUR or 29 EUR subscription before costs. Do not reuse it automatically. Any new plan needs explicit plan-specific payout rules, billing product, entitlement enforcement, downgrade rules, customer messaging and regression tests.

## Public legal information
Before commercial launch verify operator legal name, registration and tax identifiers, registered office, actual contact, privacy notice, terms, data-processing arrangements, retention and cancellation rules. Do not publish invented details or describe an existing technical privacy endpoint as a complete notice.

## Launch evidence
Validate actual Android/iOS PWA installation, QR scanning and recovery; verify behavior during connection loss. Do not claim offline transaction settlement until demonstrated. Record screenshots and test outcomes without exposing customer data.
